Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We're here to help.

What's included in the monthly price?

All plans include utilities (electricity, water), high-speed fiber internet, cleaning service, mail handling, meeting room credits, and on-site support. No hidden fees.

What are the lease terms?

We offer flexible lease terms starting from 3 months. Longer commitments (6-12 months) receive preferential rates. Month-to-month options available for select offices.

Can I access the office 24/7?

Yes! All members receive 24/7 secure key card access to the building and their private office. Perfect for teams working across different time zones.

Do you provide registered business address service?

Yes, you can use our address as your registered business address in Thailand. We also handle mail and package receiving for all members.

What's the internet speed?

We provide enterprise-grade fiber internet with speeds up to 1 Gbps, with redundant connections to ensure uninterrupted service for your business operations.

Can I book a tour before committing?

Absolutely! We encourage all prospective members to book a tour. Contact us to schedule a visit and see our facilities firsthand. Virtual tours are also available.
